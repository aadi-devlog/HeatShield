# HeatShield AI — Hackathon Demo Script

## Track 3: Industrial & Enterprise

**Duration:** ~3:00 minutes  
**Presenter:** AI Engineer / Product Lead  
**Video File:** `HeatShield_AI_Hackathon_Demo.mp4`

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 | Introduction & The Problem
**Visuals:**
- Application loads directly into the Command Center.
- Clean UI, dark mode styling (industrial feel).
- "Phoenix Logistics Hub" is displayed as the active context.

**Talk Track:**
"Welcome to HeatShield AI. For industrial and enterprise operators—like loading docks and logistics hubs—extreme heat isn't just an inconvenience, it's a massive operational risk. We're looking at the Phoenix Logistics Hub today. Our goal is to transform environmental intelligence into measurable operational decisions."

---

## 0:20 – 0:55 | Real FortyGuard Data Integration
**Visuals:**
- The presenter hovers over the Environmental Conditions panel.
- Shows live metrics: Heat Index, Wet-Bulb, Humidity.
- Highlights the Data Provenance badge confirming "Live · FortyGuard API" connection.

**Talk Track:**
"This is not simulated data. HeatShield is deeply integrated with the FortyGuard API. We are currently pulling live, hyperlocal environmental parameters specifically for the Phoenix area. You can see the exact heat index, wet-bulb temperature, and humidity driving our system in real-time."

---

## 0:55 – 1:25 | Heat Risk Analysis (Phoenix Logistics Hub)
**Visuals:**
- Navigates to the **Facility Analysis** page.
- The Heat Risk Gauge is prominent.
- Clicks the "Why?" toggle to show the 4-factor risk breakdown.

**Talk Track:**
"So what does this environment mean for our facility? HeatShield’s risk engine analyzes those FortyGuard parameters—weighting heat index, wet-bulb, humidity, and time of day—to produce an actionable risk score. It's completely transparent; operators can click 'Why?' to understand exactly how the risk is calculated."

---

## 1:25 – 2:00 | Operational Recommendation
**Visuals:**
- Scrolls down to the "What Should We Do?" and "Expected Impact" sections.
- Highlights the 4-part AI decision breakdown.

**Talk Track:**
"Knowing the risk is only step one. Step two is acting on it. HeatShield deterministic recommendation engine tells the shift manager exactly what is happening, why it matters, and precisely what action to take—in this case, shifting heavy outdoor operations to the morning window."

---

## 2:00 – 2:35 | Measurable Before / After Impact
**Visuals:**
- The presenter highlights the Before / After panel.
- Compares the "Current Plan" (13:00–16:00, 82 mins high-risk) vs "Optimized Plan" (10:00–12:00, 29 mins high-risk).
- Points to the large green metric: **"53 min saved"**.

**Talk Track:**
"This is the core value for Track 3. We show the exact measurable impact of the decision. By moving the operational window from the afternoon to the morning, HeatShield calculates a massive reduction in high-risk exposure—saving 53 minutes of danger time and protecting our 47 workers on site."

---

## 2:35 – 2:55 | Demo Mode & Heat Escalation Alert
**Visuals:**
- Presenter clicks the "Demo" toggle button.
- The amber "Simulated Data" badge appears.
- Presenter clicks "Simulate Escalation".
- Navigates to the **Alerts** page, showing a new CRITICAL red alert for a heat spike.

**Talk Track:**
"To prove the system's reactivity, we'll switch to our local Demo Mode and trigger a heat escalation. Immediately, the risk spikes, and a critical alert is pushed to the operations feed with new mitigation instructions, ensuring the site manager is never caught off guard."

---

## 2:55 – 3:10 | Closing Statement
**Visuals:**
- Returns to the Command Center.
- Hovers over the HeatShield AI branding in the header.

**Talk Track:**
"FortyGuard tells us what the environment is doing. HeatShield AI tells industrial operators exactly what to do about it. Thank you for watching our Track 3 submission."
